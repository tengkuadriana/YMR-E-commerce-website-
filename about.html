BACKGROUND OF MANAGING DIRECTOR

THE
FOUNDER.

Mr. Mohd Yusof bin Ahmad was born in 1975 in Dungun, Terengganu. He is a native of the state of Terengganu.

He obtained a Malaysian Skills Certificate (SKM) through the Low-Voltage Aerial Line course at the National Youth Skills Institute (IKBN).

He later attended the LV Generator With Synchronising course at Universiti Tenaga Nasional (UNITEN).

1995 – 1996 Electrical wireman at Sri Dungun Enterprise.
1997 – 1998 Electrical wireman at Boccard Oil & Gas Sdn. Bhd., Paka.
1998 – 1999 Electrical wireman at Equator Engineering Sdn. Bhd.
Until 2003 Returned to work at Boccard Oil & Gas in Kuantan.
MLM EXPERIENCE He entered the multi-level marketing (MLM) industry. The company profile states that he has 27 years of experience.
INTERNATIONAL DIRECTOR He achieved the rank of International Director within six months at one of the MLM companies he joined.

According to the company profile, this experience and inspiration helped form the foundation for YMR International Marketing Sdn. Bhd.
