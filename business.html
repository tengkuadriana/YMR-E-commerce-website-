HEALTH

Al-Rumman Organic Pomegranate Juice is one of the company's main products. It is made from extracts of organic pomegranate fruit and its peel.

The company also offers coffee, cocoa and goat milk beverages made with pomegranate. These beverages are blended with pomegranate extract.

The company also offers Madu Pucuk.
